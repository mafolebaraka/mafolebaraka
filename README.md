## How I built my brand on social

I started @mafolebaraka in 2021 as a student at the University of Dar es Salaam, making Swahili content about online work at a time when that subject reached Tanzanians and Kenyans in English, if at all. The first proof was a tweet offering a paid freelancing class that filled 20 one to one seats by email. Five decisions made it grow from there:

1. **Swahili first, in the street register people actually speak.** English came later, as a separate track for a global audience.
2. **A voice I call founder next door.** Personal proof before advice (I only recommend what I have used, and I had been paid by clients abroad before I taught anyone), transparency about real costs and timelines, emails written like personal letters, and strict style rules so the voice stays consistent across platforms.
3. **One editorial system, six outlets.** A single monthly theme, a daily rhythm (morning hook, midday tactical, evening community), long form written to be cut into clips, carousels and newsletter intros, and broadcast layers on WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram for people who do not want to fight an algorithm.
4. **Rented audience to owned audience.** Every free class, ebook and event moved followers onto an email list and into a community. The list's largest segments are literally the pieces of content that created them.
5. **A monetisation ladder that starts with a free class.** Free class, free toolkit, paid database or ebook, paid cohort, physical event, sponsored video and partnerships, in that order.

The audience grew from 22,000 (2022) to 50,000 and 1.5 million monthly impressions (2023) to 193,000 across Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and LinkedIn (2026). It earned the stage: a TEDx talk at the first university TEDx in Tanzania, 25+ panels and trainer engagements, a UNESCO consultation on Kiswahili in digital platforms, a Ministry of Information seminar on content monetisation, and features in TechCabal and The Economist. The full story with numbers is in [work/personal_brand.md](https://github.com/mafolebaraka/portfolio/blob/main/work/personal_brand.md).
