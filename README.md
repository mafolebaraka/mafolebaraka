# Baraka Mafole

**Digital media and social media strategist. Community builder. Bilingual content creator (English and Swahili). Based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.**

I build audiences and communities for fintechs, founders and public figures, then turn them into distribution. Over the last five years that has meant running a global community for a Y Combinator fintech, founding a freelancing education movement that now counts more than 13,000 members, and growing a personal channel past 150,000 followers across six platforms.

## What I do

**Community strategy and operations.** Circle, FluentCommunity, engagement design, member onboarding, challenge programming, community audits and AI community agents.

**Content strategy and production.** Monthly themed calendars, video show formats and run of show scripts, opinion pieces for the press, newsletters, short form vertical clips, bilingual copy.

**Influencer and creator campaigns.** Tiered creator rosters, rate card negotiation, briefs, crossover content formats for TikTok and Instagram.

**Email marketing.** Segmented lists, cohort sequences, bilingual sends, analytics audits on Flodesk, Plunk, Mailchimp and Substack.

**Digital products and platforms.** I ship the things I market: news sites, marketplaces, job boards, membership portals, ebooks and databases.
